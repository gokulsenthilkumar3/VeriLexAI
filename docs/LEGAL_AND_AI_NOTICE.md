# VeriLex AI: legal and AI-use notice

VeriLex AI supports audit, tax, and legal-compliance research workflows in India. It is a productivity tool, not a provider of regulated professional services.

## AI output

AI-generated content can be incomplete, inaccurate, or out of date. Users must validate it against source documents, current official materials, and their own professional judgment before relying on it.

## Legal research

Legal content is general information for education and compliance research. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, representation, or a guarantee of any outcome. Using VeriLex AI does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Consult a licensed advocate before taking case-specific legal action.

## Tax and audit work

Tax computations and audit workplans are preparatory aids. The responsible professional must confirm the applicable period, law, records, deductions, controls, and conclusions before filing, certifying, or issuing a report.

## Data handling

Do not upload information you are not authorised to process. Use least-privilege access, retain only necessary evidence, and configure storage and access controls to meet the organisation's obligations.
